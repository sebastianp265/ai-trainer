# AI Trainer

A personal Android app that acts as an AI personal trainer. It collects gym workouts, logged in the app, and calories eaten and burned, macros and body weight from Health Connect, whichever apps write them there, plus your preferences, and will use the Claude API to generate and adjust a flexible training and nutrition plan. All data stays on the device.
